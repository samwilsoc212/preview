Important information

Hypothetical results. Every figure comes from a backtest or simulation. No subscriber or live account earned these returns, and hypothetical results have limits, including the benefit of hindsight. Past or simulated performance is not a reliable guide to future results.

How the rules were built. The rules and their settings were developed using the same historical data shown here, which tends to overstate results. One part of the signal set lost money in its 2024 check period and did no better than chance in a statistical test. The sample is small: 46 trades in the two-year window and 62 since January 2024.

Concentration. A few large winners produce much of the return, and the table shows results without the best three trades.

Leverage. Leverage increases losses as well as gains, and you can lose all the money you commit. Leveraged crypto products may be unavailable or restricted where you live. Results assume costs of 0.10% per round trip, funding of 0.01% per 8 hours and exact fills, and live slippage may be worse, especially on tight stops.

Not advice. Signals are general and don't consider your circumstances. Cryptoassets are high risk and largely unregulated, and you are unlikely to be protected if something goes wrong.
